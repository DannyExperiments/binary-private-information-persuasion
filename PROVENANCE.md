# Provenance

Vurankaya and Topcu ask for the computational complexity of designing an optimal offline scheme for a privately informed binary-action receiver. The source treats the known-channel optimization separately from its online learning algorithm. [Original remark](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28342v1#Sx4.SSx5) · [Pinned catalog entry](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/game_theory/games-of-incomplete-information-bayesian-games.md#q587)

The recovered proof uses an anchor state and paired masses to enforce its 3-SAT tests. The manuscript retains that construction and expands exact verification. [Literature audit](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md) · [Technical disclosure](AI_DISCLOSURE.md)
