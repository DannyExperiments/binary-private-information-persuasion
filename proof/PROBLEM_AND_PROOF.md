# Statement and full proof

The rational payoff-threshold decision problem for the known-channel offline binary-action persuasion model is NP-complete. The hardness persists for action-only sender payoffs in the presence of sender-favorable ties. An inverse-polynomial additive gap rules out a deterministic fully polynomial additive approximation scheme unless P = NP.

The [paper](../paper/manuscript.pdf) and its [self-contained LaTeX source](../paper/manuscript.tex) give the entire proof. The [literature audit](../audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md) checks the original source convention and its relation to earlier hardness results.
