# Mathematical review

For the general rational instance, a weak profile's assigned value lower bounds the sender-favorable tie value. The normalized weak response regions have polynomial-bit vertices. Using compatible vertices as columns gives an exact rational linear program with a finite optimal basic solution and a polynomial certificate.

For hardness, the mandatory and Boolean test forms have coefficient magnitude at most 3. Nonnegative test likelihoods follow the coefficient signs through paired states. Singleton padding completes each state-conditional distribution and contributes a fixed baseline. The satisfying witness fits the prior, passes its guards at equality, and wins the required anchor tests. In the unsatisfiable case, equal paired priors bound total imbalance by twice the failed minus-state mass. Small-imbalance messages pass fewer tests, and the other anchor reward is absorbed by the failed guard loss. The additive threshold separation is inverse polynomial.
