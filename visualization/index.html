Where the payoff bounds come from

The two states of each pair receive equal mass in the example. Their guard signal then makes the receiver indifferent, so sender-favorable ties select action 1. In an arbitrary scheme, imbalance can increase some test scores, but the proof bounds that increase by a larger guard loss.

Each instance has a baseline C₀ from singleton signals and successful guards. Subtract that instance’s baseline from its payoff and divide by aμz, the reward from one successful test on all anchor mass. These units give certified bounds UNSAT ≤ 13/4 and SAT ≥ 4 around the threshold 7/2. The paper proves the bounds for all schemes, including imbalanced ones.
